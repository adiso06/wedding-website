Afternoon: pick your adventure

From roughly 12:30 to 6:00, choose the part of the city that sounds best and explore in smaller groups. Each route heads in a different direction, so staying within one area will leave more time for the fun part.

Chapultepec · 10-15 min west

  • Librería Porrúa, a bookstore on the water with a terrace over the lake, five minutes from the museums below.
  • Museo Nacional de Antropología, the big one. Give it at least two hours.
  • Museo de Arte Moderno, smaller and excellent, in the same park.
  • Casa Luis Barragán and Casa Gilardi, just south of the park. Both require reservations and do not accept walk-ins. Book before Sunday if either is a priority.

Centro · 20-30 min northeast

  • La Lagunilla for flea-market finds and antiques. Sunday is its best day.
  • Mercado de Artesanías La Ciudadela for crafts from across the country.
  • Biblioteca Vasconcelos, the free floating-stacks library unlike anything else in the city.
  • Palacio de Bellas Artes and MUNAL for murals and architecture, just two blocks apart.

Roma & Condesa · on foot

  • Xinú is the perfume stop to beat. Also consider Perfumerica and Lulu.
  • Clothes, bags, and local shops: Goodbye Folk, Vintage Hoe, Back to Life, La Nación de Todos, Verde Permuta, and Void. Enjoy them as a neighborhood wander, not a six-store checklist.
  • Cara de Planta, a plant shop worth the detour.
  • Or simply wander, sit in a park, and drink coffee. A slower afternoon is entirely valid.

Coyoacán · 35 min south, the committed option

  • Museo Frida Kahlo, plus the surrounding plaza and market. Timed tickets sell out days ahead, so book before Sunday. Allow 35 minutes each way and leave by 5:15.

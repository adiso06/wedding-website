Shopping plans

For Sat 19 evening and Sun 20 afternoon. Browse is for places that are fun to wander but not where you'd buy much. Buy is for places that are fun to actually shop.

Browse Look around

Beautiful stores, great streets, window-shopping pace.

SoHo

Flagships and boutiques along Broadway and on Prince, Spring, Greene and Mercer Streets. Worth a stop: Apple SoHo, the MoMA Design Store (open until 7) and Housing Works Bookstore Cafe.

Feels like Cast-iron buildings, cobblestone side streets, luxury logos in every window, and a crowd. Beautiful, and pricey enough that it stays browsing.

  • W from Queensboro Plaza to Prince St (weekdays)
  • R from Queens Plaza (any day)

Williamsburg

Independent shops along Bedford Ave, and Artists & Fleas (70 N 7th St), an indoor market of local makers and vintage sellers, open Fri–Sun 11–7. End at Marsha P. Johnson State Park on the water for the skyline.

Feels like Young and scruffy-stylish: old warehouses, murals, coffee shops, vintage racks on the sidewalk. Cheaper and more relaxed than SoHo.

  • G from Court Sq to Nassau Av, then walk down Bedford Ave
  • Home: NYC Ferry from North Williamsburg to Hunters Point South

Buy Actually shop

Fun places where bags actually get filled.

Canal Street and Century 21

Knockoff bags, sunglasses and souvenirs along Canal St near Broadway. Bring cash and bargain. The better bags usually aren't on display, so ask, stay together, and walk away if a back room feels off. San Gennaro is a block over on Mulberry St through Sep 27. Then take the R two stops to Cortlandt St for Century 21, the discount designer department store.

Feels like Loud, crowded and chaotic, sellers calling from doorways, Chinatown on one side and Little Italy's festival on the other. Keep bags zipped and in front.

  • NW from Queensboro Plaza to Canal St
  • Century 21: Mon–Sat 9–9, Sun 11–8

Nordstrom Rack and Macy's

Nordstrom Rack Union Square, then Macy's Herald Square, the giant flagship a few stops back up the same line.

Feels like Ordinary big-city shopping, in the best way: bright, busy, endless racks, and a store that takes up a whole city block.

  • NW to 14 St–Union Sq
  • Rack: Mon–Sat 10–9, Sun 11–7
  • Macy's: Mon–Thu 10–9, Fri–Sat 10–10, Sun 11–9
